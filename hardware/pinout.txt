=====================================================================
OMNISTRIKE HARDWARE PINOUT DEFINITION (ESP32-S3 Dev Module)
=====================================================================

1. BNO085 IMU SENSOR CONECTIONS (I2C Architecture)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
ESP32-S3 Pin      | BNO085 Sensor Pin | Purpose
---------------------------------------------------------------------
3.3V              | VCC               | Power Supply
GND               | GND               | Ground Reference
GPIO 21           | SDA               | I2C Serial Data Line
GPIO 22           | SCL               | I2C Serial Clock Line

2. HAPTIC FEEDBACK MOTOR
---------------------------------------------------------------------
ESP32-S3 Pin      | Motor Controller  | Purpose
---------------------------------------------------------------------
GPIO 15           | PWM / IN (+)      | Haptic Signal Logic (High/Low)
GND               | (-)               | Common Ground Reference
